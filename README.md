# Pharm Flash Cards — ATI RN Pharmacology 9.0

Flash card site for ATI RN Pharmacology for Nursing, Ed. 9.0. No build step and no dependencies. It works on a phone.

**Live:** https://wilybrave.github.io/pharm-study/

Every card covers **indications, main side effects, adverse effects, contraindications, interactions, special warnings, client education, and antidote** (if there is one).

## Modes

- **Flash cards:** the front shows the drug and its class. Tap to flip and see everything. Mark each card *Again* (it comes back a few cards later) or *Got it*. When you finish a deck, you can redo only the ones you missed.
- **Drill one topic:** the front asks one thing, such as "Antidote?" or "Contraindications?". Use *Mixed* to get a random topic each card, or pick one topic and run it across every drug. *Antidote only* gives a quick list of antidotes.
- **Browse / print:** every card laid out by chapter, with a search box. Print from the browser.

Keyboard: Space flips, ← and → move between cards, 1 = Again, 2 = Got it. On a phone, swipe left or right.
Progress is saved in the browser.
